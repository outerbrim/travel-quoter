How the fee is worked out. Your drive time is split into the included part and the extra part. The same share of the kilometres becomes chargeable, so a slow mountain road and a fast freeway are treated fairly.

Vehicle cost = chargeable km for both legs × (fuel per km + wear and tear). Travel time = extra hours for both legs × your hourly rate. Accommodation and tolls are added at cost.

Accommodation. Prices swing with season and location, so check a real listing near the venue for the wedding date and round up a little. Charge it at cost. If the couple would rather book your room themselves, set nights to 0.
